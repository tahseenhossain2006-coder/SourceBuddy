01 · How it works

Three steps. Zero portals.

01

Describe it once

One plain sentence — product, quantity, where it needs to land. No category trees, no RFQ templates, no account portals. If a colleague on the dock could understand it, the engine can.

02

The engine sounds the depths

Your request is parsed into specs and cast across a mapped network of suppliers — cross-checking price history, certifications, capacity, and lead times in parallel. That's the two seconds.
